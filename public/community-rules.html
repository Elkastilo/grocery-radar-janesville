Community Rules

Grocery Radar Janesville works best when local shoppers submit honest, useful reports.

Submit Honest Prices

Only submit prices you personally saw in Janesville. Do not submit fake reports, guessed prices, scraped prices, or copied data from outside the app.

Be Respectful

No abusive usernames, spam, harassment, or inappropriate uploads.

Use Proof When Possible

Receipt, shelf tag, and weekly ad photos help admin and other users trust a report.

Moderation

Admin may remove reports, reject photos, reset points, suspend accounts, or ban accounts for abuse.
