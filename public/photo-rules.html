Photo Upload Rules

Photo uploads are for grocery price proof and admin moderation only.

Allowed Proof

Upload receipt photos, shelf tag photos, or weekly ad photos/screenshots that help verify a grocery price.

Protect Privacy

Avoid faces. Cover payment information, card numbers, loyalty numbers, addresses, phone numbers, and other private details on receipts.

Do Not Upload

Do not upload private documents, medical documents, IDs, unrelated photos, spam, or anything unsafe or abusive.

Admin Review

Admin may reject blurry, unreadable, unrelated, or unsafe uploads.
