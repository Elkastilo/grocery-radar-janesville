Price Disclaimer

Grocery Radar Janesville shows user-submitted grocery price reports. It shows the cheapest reported price, not a guaranteed cheapest price.

Prices Can Change

Store prices, sales, clearance deals, and availability can change quickly. A report may be outdated by the time you shop.

Check Before Buying

Always check the shelf tag, receipt, weekly ad, store app, or store staff before buying. Do not rely only on the app for final purchase decisions.

Admin Review

Admin review helps reduce bad reports, but it does not guarantee that a price is current or available.
