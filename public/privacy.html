Privacy Policy

Grocery Radar Janesville is an independent price-information service for Janesville, Wisconsin. A public shopper account is not required.

What The App Collects

Anonymous proof submissions receive a high-entropy tracking capability stored by the shopper's browser. Grocery Radar stores submitted proof images and their review outcomes. Staff accounts are separate and protected. Historical account-linked records remain supported.

Search Demand

Search demand is aggregated by normalized query, result count, counts, and time. It is used to identify missing products. Grocery Radar does not attach a shopper account, email, full IP address, or device fingerprint to this aggregate search-demand record.

Photos

Uploaded photos may be stored privately for proof and moderation. Proof originals are not public product images. Users should not upload faces, payment information, private documents, medical documents, or anything unrelated to grocery price proof.

How Data Is Used

Data is used to operate the app, review submissions, show approved grocery price reports, aggregate unmet product demand, and prevent abuse. Anonymous shopping lists and submission references are stored on the shopper's device.

Beta Privacy

This page describes the current product architecture. It is operational product information and may require formal legal review before broad commercial launch.

Local Sponsors

Local sponsor cards may appear in the app. We do not sell personal user data to sponsors. Sponsor stats are anonymous aggregate counts, such as views or interest taps.
