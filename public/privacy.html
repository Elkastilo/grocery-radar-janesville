Privacy Policy

Grocery Radar Janesville is a beta app for crowdsourced grocery price reports in Janesville, Wisconsin.

What The App Collects

The app collects account information such as username and email address. It stores grocery price reports, verifications, notes, store requests, suggestions, and uploaded proof photos that users submit.

Photos

Uploaded photos may be stored for proof and moderation. Users should not upload faces, payment information, private documents, medical documents, or anything unrelated to grocery price proof.

How Data Is Used

Data is used to operate the app, review submissions, show approved grocery price reports, manage accounts, and prevent abuse.

Beta Privacy

Grocery Radar Janesville does not sell personal data in this beta wording. Future versions should add a full privacy review before public launch.

Local Sponsors

Local sponsor cards may appear in the app. We do not sell personal user data to sponsors. Sponsor stats are anonymous aggregate counts, such as views or interest taps.
