How to submit proof Quick help and examples

Use this page when you have a receipt, shelf tag, weekly ad, or store link. Pick the store, choose the proof type, upload the image or add the link, and add item or price hints only if you want. Admin reviews the proof before any price appears publicly.

  • Receipt photo means a picture of your receipt.
  • Shelf tag photo means a picture of the price tag at the store.
  • Store page/source link means a weekly ad, deal page, or product page.
  • Item, price, brand, and size are hints only. Add what you know.
